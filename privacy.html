BANDCALL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

BandCall is a live music request service that allows performers to manage songs, saved set lists and live gigs, and allows audience members to send song requests, requests to sing and shout-outs.

Information we collect

When a performer creates a BandCall account, we process the email address used to create and sign in to that account.

Performers may also add information including act names, song libraries, saved set lists, gig information and request-management information.

Audience members do not need to create a BandCall account. When an audience member submits something during a live BandCall session, we may process the name they enter, the song they select, the type of request and any message or shout-out they choose to send.

How we use information

We use this information to operate BandCall, including authenticating performer accounts, storing performer content, running live gig sessions, displaying available songs, delivering audience requests to performers and maintaining the reliability and security of the service.

Service providers

BandCall uses Supabase for services including authentication, database hosting and server-side functionality. Information processed through BandCall may therefore be stored or processed using Supabase infrastructure.

Audience access and live sessions

Audience members can access a live BandCall session using a QR code or link supplied by the performer.

The audience page is designed to expose only information needed for the live session, such as the gig name and songs currently available for requests.

Data retention

Performer account data remains associated with the BandCall account until it is deleted or is no longer required to provide the service.

Gig and request information may remain stored as part of the performer's BandCall account until that account is deleted or the information is otherwise removed.

Account deletion

Performers can initiate deletion of their BandCall account from the Settings screen inside the BandCall app.

Account deletion removes the BandCall data associated with that account and deletes the corresponding authentication account.

Children

BandCall is not designed for the collection of unnecessary personal information from children. Audience participants should avoid entering sensitive or unnecessary personal information into messages or shout-outs.

Security

BandCall uses authenticated access controls and server-side operations intended to restrict access to performer account information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Performers can manage their BandCall content through the app and can delete their account using the in-app account deletion feature.
