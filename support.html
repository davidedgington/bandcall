EMAIL SUPPORT

For performers

If you cannot sign in, create a gig, load songs or set lists, display your audience QR code, or manage live requests, email us with the exact error message you see.

For audience members

Audience members do not need a BandCall account or app. Scan the performer's QR code or open the link they provide.

If a BandCall session says that it is not live, ask the performer to confirm that the gig is still active.

Account deletion

Performers can delete their BandCall account from the Settings screen inside the app.

If you cannot access the app and need assistance with your account, contact BandCall Support using the email address above.
